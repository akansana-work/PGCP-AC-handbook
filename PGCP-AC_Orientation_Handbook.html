Chapter 04

How we communicate

We run on a simple principle: every message already has the details you need. A class meeting is called whenever something needs more clarity, and attendance there is mandatory. So your job is easy — read carefully, the first time.

Before you ask

  • Recheck the notice group
  • Ask a classmate
  • Message during suitable hours
  • Introduce yourself clearly and say what you need

Avoid this

  • Re-asking something already shared, after being late or absent
  • Sending just "hello" and waiting
  • Messaging late at night and expecting an instant reply

Faculty have families and lives outside class too — a late-night message may simply wait until morning. That's not neglect, it's balance.

Chapter 05

Your profile, your identity

  • Set your real name and a clear photo as your WhatsApp DP — required for the whole course.
  • When you message someone for the first time, introduce yourself: name, batch, and what you need. We may not know you personally yet — keep it professional.
  • Save all CDAC Mumbai staff contact numbers in your phone as soon as you receive them, so faculty messages don't land as an unknown number.
  • Make your DP visible to your contacts: WhatsApp Settings → Privacy → Profile photo → set to "My contacts" (not "Nobody"). Staff need to recognize you at a glance.

Requesting leave

One process, every time — WhatsApp messages don't count as a leave request.

02

Send to lab-kharghar@cdac.in, addressed to Course Coordinator Mr. Vipul Tembulwar, with adityakansana@cdac.in in CC.

03

Attach supporting proof — doctor's note, appointment slip, ticket, admit card. No document, no approval.

04

Only 1–2 days may be granted at a time.

05

Your leave is valid only once you get a reply. No reply means it isn't approved yet.

Don't message the notice group asking for leave — it has to be this email, every time.
Your email should include
  • A clear subject line — e.g. "Leave request – [Your full name], PGCP-AC Aug'26"
  • Your full name and batch, right at the start of the email body
  • The exact date(s) you need leave for
  • The reason for leave, stated plainly
  • The supporting document attached — mention in the email that it's attached, and actually attach it
Chapter 09

Quick answers

Q

I missed a session — can I ask what was said?
Check the notice group and ask a classmate first. Repeating shared information isn't fair to whoever already read it.

Q

Can I request leave over WhatsApp?
No — email only, to the address in Chapter 8.

Q

Can I join a session on my phone?
No — a laptop is mandatory, with your camera on throughout.

Q

Why do I need a Zoom account just to join a link?
Every session runs on a recurring Zoom link that requires registration. You must have a Zoom account created with your @cmaug26 Gmail ID, sign in to Zoom with it, and then register on the link you're given. Try to join without registering first, and you'll hit an authentication error.

Q

Where does the actual join link show up?
Once you register, Zoom emails the join link to the same registered Gmail ID — it will arrive, so check your spam folder and make sure your Gmail storage isn't full. Star that email so you can find it again quickly.

Q

Is it the same link for every module?
No — each module gets its own separate recurring link, so register fresh for each one.

Q

Is the recording shared if I miss a session?
Yes, lecture recordings are shared in the Notice Group — but processing a full session's recording takes time, so have some patience. The recording is not a substitute for attending: attendance is mandatory for both live sessions and lab sessions, whether or not you plan to watch the recording later.
